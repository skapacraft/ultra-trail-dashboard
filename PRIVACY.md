## The short version

Ultra-Trail Dashboard runs entirely on your Garmin device. It doesn't transmit anything to any server, doesn't require an internet connection, and doesn't collect any personal, location, or health data beyond the normal operation of the Garmin Connect ecosystem you've already chosen to use.

## What data the app reads

While a running or trail running activity is in progress, Ultra-Trail Dashboard reads the following from your Garmin device, made available by the system through Garmin's standard Connect IQ API (`Activity.Info`):

- Current speed and pace
- Current heart rate
- Altitude and distance travelled
- Device system settings (unit preferences, screen shape, colour theme)

## What the app does with this data

All calculations, including smoothed grade and grade-adjusted pace, happen entirely on device, in real time, purely to be displayed on screen during the activity.

The values the app produces are written into the activity's .FIT file, the same file Garmin Connect already generates for any workout. From there it follows the same sync path you have already configured with Garmin Connect, and, if linked, Strava or any other third-party service of your own choosing.

## What the app does not do

- It does not transmit any data to external servers, ours or third party.
- It does not collect, store, or share any personal, location, or health information beyond the normal operation of the Garmin device and the Garmin Connect ecosystem you have already chosen to use.
- It does not use any third-party analytics, advertising tracking, or telemetry services.
- It requires no account, login, or internet connection to function.

## User settings

User-configurable settings, such as the grade smoothing window, are stored locally on the device through Connect IQ's standard Properties mechanism, and never leave the device.
